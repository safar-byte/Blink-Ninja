# Eye Open Time Tracker

Simple browser-based tool that uses your webcam and MediaPipe FaceMesh to measure how long your eyes stay open before the first blink/close.

## How to run

1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser (Chrome recommended).
2. Allow camera access when the browser prompts you.
3. Click **Start**, look at the camera, and keep your eyes open.
4. When you blink or close your eyes, the page will show your total eye-open time.

> If you see camera permission issues, check your browser settings and Windows privacy settings to ensure the browser is allowed to use the camera.
